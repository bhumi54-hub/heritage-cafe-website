Relax at Our Heritage Café in the Heart of Fontainhas, Panjim

Escape the afternoon Goa heat and step into our restored Portuguese-style home. Nestled within Panjim's colorful Latin Quarter, our heritage café in Fontainhas offers travelers and remote workers a quiet, air-conditioned sanctuary with fresh artisanal coffee and fast Wi-Fi.

Artisanal Coffee & Fresh Goan Bakery Items

Whether you need a morning espresso before exploring the historic streets or an afternoon sweet treat, our kitchen serves daily freshly baked items like croissants, Goan poi sandwiches, and traditional Bebinca. You can explore our full café menu and daily opening hours before stopping by.

Visit Us in Fontainhas

We package signature locally roasted coffee beans to take home as souvenirs. Located along the walking tour route through the Latin Quarter, check our Panjim café location map and table reservations to plan your visit.
